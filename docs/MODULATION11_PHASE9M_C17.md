## 2. Finite analytic WEAK population upper envelopes — exact scope

Define `A = E_WEAK Σ_{all 78,624 intraday substeps, assets} q_{a,k} S_{a,k}` for predictable funded long inventory, and `U = E_WEAK Σ_{fills} (signed beneficial execution credit relative to an actually observed eight-step reference)_+` under C15's decomposition. Actual native fill-to-step equivalence is not proved.

Under explicitly assumed bar-open-funded acquisitions, no intrabar reacquisition or borrowing, nonnegative fees, sales no higher than the original bar's actual eight-step high, and a tradeable reference inside the eight-step low-high support, the maximum across a bar has a uniform Gaussian positive-part bound. This makes the *conditional analytic* population expectation finite without any Monte Carlo sampling, even though the 72-hour Gaussian price ratio has unbounded support.

For each asset, let `X_{a,k}` be the log-return at substep prefix `k`. The actual M9 WEAK generator uses sigma at its registered base times 1.25 (WEAK) and a final support multiplier up to 1.2 and daily activity up to exp(0.7), with a 0.004 probability of a 4x sigma bar. For delta bounded between ±0.000025*1.1 per bar, define:

    kplus_a = Σ_{k=1..8} E[(exp(X_{a,k})-1)_+] under maximum positive drift
    kminus_a = Σ_{k=1..8} E[(1-exp(X_{a,k}))_+] under maximum negative drift
    kplus = max_a kplus_a; r = max_a (kplus_a + kminus_a)
    K = 1+kplus; N = 126*78; G = (K^N-1)/(K-1).

The positive and negative payoff expectations are computed analytically for the registered Gaussian mixture, with independent Simpson quadrature and call-put parity tests. The union-of-positive-parts inequality dominates max-price and range without assuming asset independence. Original WEAK overnight jumps are mean-one and there are no overnight trades in the conditional policy class. The financed wealth tower gives:

    E_WEAK[A] <= 8 * 20,000 * K * G
    E_WEAK[U] <= 20,000 * r * G

subject to the hypotheses above. The 12% gross figure is only an original ADMISSION constraint; it is never used as continuous exposure.

## 4. Decision

**N9-S = NO_GO**. C17 resolves *recoverability* of the original generator's ordered price tape for a pre-existing development seed and proves finite, policy-class-conditional WEAK population upper envelopes. Because those envelopes are orders of magnitude too large and complete native assumptions remain unproven, it is not an economic impossibility certificate. The pending mathematical task is a tighter causal, inventory-conditioned expectation bound linked to independently verifiable actual native receipts and first-cross execution, with no retrospective re-selection, no final accounts and no change to original costs/hurdles.
