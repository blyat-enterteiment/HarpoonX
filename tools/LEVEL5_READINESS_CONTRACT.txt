HARPOON — economic gate v2 / level 5 evidence bridge

Scope
Three focused corrections: (1) bridge the complete funded comparison to the
economic evaluator, (2) preserve event risk, chronological costs and one frozen
stress comparison, (3) bind preregistration and search history to actual files
and the preserved Quant journal. This is offline research. No model fitting,
search reservation, broker call, deployment or LIVE authority occurs.

Commands from the full application root
  node tools/level5_readiness.cjs --input request.json --output derived.json
  node tools/level5_readiness.cjs --input request.json --verify derived.json --output verification.json
  cd quant
  python -m harpoon_quant.economic_gate --protocol protocol.json --input evidence.json --output economic-report.json

The Python command accepts legacy HARPOON_ECONOMIC_PROTOCOL_1 unchanged. V1 C0
still means cash/no-entry. Only HARPOON_ECONOMIC_PROTOCOL_2 can emit score=5.
Its control roles must be exactly:
  SVR: CANDIDATE
  RIDGE: FIXED_BENCHMARK
  NATIVE: PRODUCER_C0
  CASH: DETERMINISTIC_NO_ENTRY

Node API
  deriveLevel5(request, baseDirectory)
  verifyLevel5Derivation(request, report, baseDirectory)

Derivation request
  schema: HARPOON_LEVEL5_DERIVATION_REQUEST_1
  level4:
    request: null | {path,sha256}
  stress:
    - id: frozen scenario identifier
      request: {path,sha256}
      report: {path,sha256}

References use raw-byte SHA-256. They are relative to the JSON containing them.
The level4 request retains its existing data/comparison/ledger contract and is
reevaluated, not trusted from a supplied score. Its comparison request/report
are reexecuted by the existing coordinator and fixed-estimator verifier.
Caller-supplied curves/policies are rejected. Missing resources remain null.
The empty request above with stress:[] is executable and returns exact blockers.

Stress scope and budget
At most one additional three-policy comparison fits the existing six-slot
family. The base/stress historical registrations must be real verified adjacent
three-slot reservations in the same existing journal. No slots are reserved by
this verifier. Synthetic test fixtures perform no journal reservation and remain
financially ineligible. CASH is an exact deterministic no-entry reference using
the same capital and external costs, not another searched strategy or slot.

Only nondecreasing fees_bps_per_side, fee_per_fill_usd and slippage_bps may differ
between base and stress, apart from request id and registration receipt. At least
one must increase. Models and original model bytes, all market/events/signals,
calendar, initial capital, risk and external cost amount must match. No claimed
stress result bypasses complete reexecution. Latency/liquidity stress is not
implemented by this bounded adapter; it does not fabricate those distributions.

Chronological account and economic equity
Account equity and fills remain those of the original coordinator replay.
The verified ledger's external cost entries are applied at their documented
accrual timestamps. They must sum to the external amount in the funded request.
Trading fees are already in cash and are never subtracted again. An inserted
cost event carries the last observed account/inventory mark and labels that
fact; it does not invent a fresh quote. Its real timestamp contributes to risk,
and its return belongs to the next observed session interval. Weekends and
after-hours costs do not create new independent market sessions.

Every event reconciles cash + inventory = account equity and account equity -
external costs = economic equity. Zero/negative equity at any event, unpaid
economic cash, peak exposure, drawdown, stale terminal marks and observed holding
duration remain visible even when the daily close recovers. The evaluator uses
the maximum observed event risk and time weights last-observed exposure between
events. Unobserved price paths are not certified. CASH earns no invented interest
and has no transaction fees because it never trades; it pays all shared external
costs. No terminal forced close or price interpolation is introduced.

Financing contract
The producer can explicitly reserve cash with execution_model.operating_cash_reserve:
  amount_usd: fixed reserve R
  known_at: observed/frozen timestamp before the replay start
  method: FIXED_INITIAL_CASH_RESERVE
The original cash/NAV remain unchanged; every policy's buying power and
non-marginable buying power equal cash-R. The original coordinator reexecutes
its sizing and fills with that reserve, and the v2 evaluator checks those fields
at every snapshot. R is held for the entire test, a conservative assumption,
and must cover all external costs. Costs are subtracted from economic NAV once
when accrued, not again when reserving cash. CASH shares this reserve.

If nonzero costs lack this original shared reserve or its reconstruction,
EXTERNAL_COST_CAPITAL_RESERVE_PARITY_UNVERIFIED remains explicit. If economic
cash cannot cover accrued costs, EXTERNAL_COST_FINANCING_NOT_AVAILABLE is
recorded. If cash falls below the still-reserved R, RESERVED_CASH_DEFICIT is
recorded even when total NAV stays positive. No invented broker debit, capital
contribution or sale of unexecuted inventory funds a cost. The model reserves
existing capital; this contract does not authorize additional spending/exposure.

V2 protocol
All v1 chronology, unit, risk, economic minimum, dependence, purge/embargo,
bootstrap and search validations remain. Replace minimum_delta_c0_log_growth by
minimum_delta_native_log_growth and add:
  schema: HARPOON_ECONOMIC_PROTOCOL_2
  policy_roles: exact roles above
  exposure_measure: EVENT_INTERVAL_TIME_WEIGHTED_LAST_OBSERVED
  external_cost_method: VERIFIED_LEDGER_ACCRUAL_TIMESTAMPS
  stress_scenarios: exactly one item
    id: exact scenario id
    execution_overrides: exact frozen increased execution fields
    minimum_net_pnl_usd: nonnegative, fixed before test
    minimum_delta_ridge_usd: nonnegative, fixed before test
    max_drawdown_fraction: positive and no higher than base risk limit

The Node bindings output gives the formulas for policy_hash, risk_hash,
cost_hash and code_hash. The policy hash includes exact model-byte hashes;
risk binds the full coordinator configuration. The cost hash binds execution
assumptions and the verified-accrual accounting method, not unknowable future
realized cost totals. Code includes all executed funded sources plus both level4
and level5 adapters; bridge hashes are captured before and after reexecution.
Actual future receipts, totals and market input remain bound in the report.

This is a schema, not a ready financial protocol. Unknown dates, adequate block
lengths, economic minimums, cost assumptions and risk limits cannot be copied
from synthetic tests. Register the real protocol and model bytes before the
START of the final test. A later re-download or first_access declaration cannot
convert old historical results into a new test. Local clocks/hash chains do not
establish external time attestation or independent model provenance.

  liveApproved: false

  history_manifest: raw-byte artifact reference described below

The Python evaluator invokes only the fixed local Node adapter and consumes its
newly derived output. No exported report, booleans or caller curves substitute
for reconstruction. It reuses paired_block_bootstrap without changing its
Bonferroni family (attempts × block lengths × three endpoints), sampling unit,
tail-resolution/compute bounds, degeneracy checks or confidence limitations.
Its external endpoint name is svr_minus_native, not legacy cash C0.

Historical manifest
  schema: HARPOON_RESEARCH_HISTORY_MANIFEST_2
  sources:
    quant_journal: {path,sha256}
    ultimate_registry: {path,sha256}
    tp32_report: {path,sha256}
    prior_readiness: {path,sha256}
  quant_journal_wal: optional bound WAL reference, mandatory if nonempty
  quant_audit_head: current existing journal head
  historical_source_files: references keyed by relative_path in the prior audit
  global_deduplicated_total: null until identities are actually reconciled
  attempt_identity_map: null until source records support it

The prior audit is anchored to the retained 30.10.1 bytes; Ultimate and TP32
references must match its exact source hashes. The Quant journal is opened
read-only, its audit chain verified, and the retained 23-slot prefix must be
present. A coherent unrelated replacement journal fails that prefix binding.
Every historical source named by the prior audit must also be provided by hash.
Known prior data periods are extracted and overlap is checked. Unknown history
coverage remains a blocker; histories are not silently summed as independent
trials. The retained Quant prefix itself is reconstructed local evidence, not
an externally authenticated research diary.

If native records eventually support cross-family identities, each map entry
must cover one recorded family slot and identify an actual immutable source
record via JSON pointer (and journal_key for QUANT). The source record must
contain attempt_id, code_hash, data_hash and policy_hash. Every known family
slot must be covered once; reuse of a source location is rejected. Only identical
native identities deduplicate. A claimed total is compared to the derived count.
Current historical artifacts do not establish that map: keep total and map null.

Outcome
Level 4 must be reconstructed on these exact inputs. Level 5 additionally needs
positive absolute net economic PnL, all corrected one-sided lower bounds above
zero/economic minimums for SVR and its Ridge/native comparisons, comparable
exposure, every risk/financing/freshness/history condition and the preregistered
stress criterion. Missing evidence remains VENTAJA_NO_DEMOSTRADA; demonstrably
negative economics or excessive candidate risk is RECHAZADO. The sole favorable
verdict is CANDIDATO_PARA_VALIDACION_FORWARD. All execution, LIVE and financial
authority flags stay false. Neither a score nor software tests guarantee profit.
